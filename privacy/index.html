Just Tell Me, Bru!

Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Just Tell Me, Bru! is a free browser extension that rewrites clickbait headlines on mybroadband.co.za and businesstech.co.za into factual ones. This page explains what it does with data. The short version: it doesn't collect anything about you.

What the extension does

  • It runs only on mybroadband.co.za and businesstech.co.za.
  • It reads the headlines and article links on those pages, inside your browser, to match them against a list of rewritten headlines.
  • It downloads that list, a public file at bru.011.sh, at most every ten minutes and only while you have one of those sites open. The whole list is downloaded and matched on your device, so the server never learns which articles you look at.

What it stores

On your own device only, using your browser's extension storage:

  • your settings: whether rewriting is on, whether the original headline shows underneath, and whether sponsored posts are dimmed
  • the last downloaded copy of the headlines list, kept in temporary memory so it isn't downloaded again on every page (it's cleared when you close the browser)

Removing the extension removes all of this.

What it doesn't do

  • It doesn't collect your name, email, browsing history or anything else about you.
  • It doesn't use analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • It doesn't send anything from the pages you visit to me or anyone else.
  • It doesn't sell or share data, because it has none to sell or share.

The headlines file

When the extension downloads the headlines list, your browser makes an ordinary web request to the server hosting it, like visiting any website. The hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security and reliability. I don't use those logs to identify or track anyone.

This website

The page at bru.011.sh counts visits with Simple Analytics, which doesn't use cookies or collect personal data. That only applies to the web pages. The extension never loads it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change and the new version will be posted here.
