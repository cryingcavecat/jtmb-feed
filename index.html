Just Tell Me, Bru!

Clickbait is kak. Here’s what actually happened.

Just Tell Me, Bru! is a free Chrome extension that rewrites clickbait headlines on MyBroadband and BusinessTech into what the article actually says. The site’s original headline stays underneath, and sponsored posts get labelled.

Get it: Add it to Chrome (free).

Why I made it: the write-up, with a game to test how well you can guess what a headline is hiding.

This address also serves the extension’s headlines file. The extension collects nothing about you, and this page only counts visits, without cookies: see the privacy policy.
